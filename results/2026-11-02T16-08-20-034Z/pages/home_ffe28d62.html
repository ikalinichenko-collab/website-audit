What Is an Applied AI Company?

An applied AI company helps teams use AI in real production workflows, not just in demos, pilots, or strategy decks.

For software companies, that means putting AI into the codebase and development process while measuring whether it actually improves delivery.

GoGloby does this three ways: we recruit the AI leaders who own it, we embed the forward-deployed engineers who ship it, and we run the AI Intelligence Layer that proves it against your own baseline.

The Applied AI Engineering Gaps We Solve

Five problems often appear when established software companies start using AI. The first step is identifying which one is actually limiting the result.

Uneven AI Adoption

Some engineers use AI throughout their workflow while others barely use the tools they have. Buying licenses does not guarantee adoption. The AI Intelligence Layer shows where AI is actually being used and where adoption is still limited.

Unstable AI Delivery

AI can help engineers write code faster without helping the team ship faster. More changes enter review, reviewers become the bottleneck, and no one knows whether AI-generated output is safe to merge. The goal is to improve the whole workflow, not just code generation.

Uncontrolled AI Spend

AI spending can grow quickly across Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and model usage. The problem is knowing which spending contributes to shipped work and which spending produces little value.

Invisible AI ROI

Companies spend more on AI, but many still cannot show what changed in delivery. GoGloby connects AI ROI to shipped work and makes it visible per feature, per team, per developer, and per dollar against the company’s starting baseline.

No Number For The Board

Everyone works with AI, budgets are approved, and when the board asks what it returned there is nothing to show. Hours and story points say nothing about AI. The Layer gives the CTO one chart against the company’s own baseline.

No Access to Proven AI Talent

Engineers who already run an agentic SDLC on production code are a few thousand people, and labs and hyperscalers compete for every one. They’re not on job boards, and a mid-market offer rarely wins. GoGloby embeds them nearshore, forward-deployed in under 4 weeks.

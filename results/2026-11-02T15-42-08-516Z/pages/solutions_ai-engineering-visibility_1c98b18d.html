What Is AI Cost Management?

AI cost management is the practice of tracking, attributing, forecasting, and optimizing AI spend across models, tools, teams, and workflows, tied to the engineering output it produced.

For GoGloby, the unit that matters is cost per shipped feature. The AI Intelligence Layer joins your AI spend to your actual work, so every dollar is attributed to a story, PR, model call, team, and the shipped feature it supported.

AI Cost Management vs. Cloud FinOps

Cloud FinOps tracks infrastructure and cloud services. AI cost management also has to see model calls, tokens, prompts, AI coding sessions, PRs, CI runs, stories, and shipped features, none of which appear on a cloud bill.

AI Cost Management vs. Engineering Dashboards

Engineering dashboards report velocity and cycle time, which are activity, not cost. AI cost management prices the work in dollars and ties it to what actually shipped.

THE PROBLEM

Why AI Cost Management Breaks Inside Engineering Teams

Agentic Output Is Measured. Its Cost Isn’t.

AI Spend Is Untraceable at Source

LLM invoices aggregate at the org level. Token usage inside Claude Code, Cursor, and Copilot sessions is never joined to the commits, PRs, or features it produced.

No Join Key From Feature to Model Calls

Jira and Linear track points and cycle time but never see tokens. Without session-to-feature attribution, cost per shipped feature is not a computable metric.

Model Mix Is Unmanaged

Expensive models routinely execute tasks a cheaper model could handle. With a large unit-price gap between model tiers and no per-task telemetry, routing waste is structurally invisible.

AI Cost Anomalies Surface Too Late

Retry loops, cache misses, and long-context blowups appear as line items 30 days later, not as same-day signals with a trace ID attached.

WHAT IT SHOW

AI Cost Management Metrics Engineering Leaders Track

What Engineering Leaders Measure, Sprint by Sprint 

Cost Per Shipped Story

Ties the full AI cost of each completed story, from model calls to the PRs that closed it, back to the story itself. This is your core unit of value, the number that makes AI spend comparable across teams and sprints.

AI-Authored PR Rework

Tracks how much AI-generated code gets reopened, reverted, or rewritten after it first merges. High rework means you are paying twice for the same feature and shows where AI output is not yet production-ready.

Model Mix

Shows which models ran which tasks and what each tier cost against the work it produced. Expensive models routinely handle jobs a cheaper tier could do, and seeing the mix per task is how you catch that routing waste early.

Plan Versus Actual Spend

 Compares forecasted AI spend to what each team actually consumed, sprint by sprint. It closes the gap between the budget you committed and the invoice you receive, and gives you next quarter’s number before you commit it.

Cost Per Merged PR

Attributes AI cost to every pull request that reaches main. It prices real engineering output at the PR level, so you can see which teams and workflows turn AI spend into shipped code most efficiently.

Failed CI Cost

Captures the AI and compute spend burned on pipeline runs that never passed. Failed CI produces nothing shippable, so tracking it surfaces the retry loops and flaky tests that quietly inflate your bill.

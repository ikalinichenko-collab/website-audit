Solving the AI Engineering Bottleneck

Why We Built GoGloby

Founded in 2021, GoGloby spent its first years building engineering teams for some of the most demanding companies in the US. In 2025 we made a deliberate shift, from traditional staffing to Applied AI Engineering, turning engineering into AI-native production and proving the return against your own baseline.

The engineering leaders we had worked with for years kept hitting the same problems. They could not find engineers who actually shipped AI in production. Their teams used AI without standards, so output was inconsistent and hard to govern. They had no proof to take to the board. And every new AI tool looked like a security risk they could not afford.

So we built one offer with 3 deliverables that only work together. The AI Intelligence Layer, deployed inside your VPC, joins every AI dollar to the work it produced and shows cost and return on every shipped feature. Forward-Deployed Engineers work inside your team: an Architect installs the Agentic SDLC so there is one way of working with AI, and Applied AI Engineers ship features at AI speed against your own baseline.

And ROI that grows every month, as the AI Intelligence Layer cuts waste, the engineers fix what it surfaces, and each fix becomes next month’s baseline. Everything runs inside your perimeter, with your access rules.

Our mission is to turn engineering into AI-native production for established software companies: every AI dollar visible, features shipping faster against your own baseline, 30 to 40% lower cost, and one number the CTO can take to the board.

Trusted by Hasbro, TATA, EverCommerce, and 100+ companies across 10+ industries.

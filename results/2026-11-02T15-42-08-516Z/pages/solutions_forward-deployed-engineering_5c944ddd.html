THE ENGAGEMENT MODEL

What Is Forward-Deployed Engineering?

Forward-deployed engineering, often shortened to FDE, is an embedded engineering model where senior engineers work inside your company, not from the outside. Unlike a consultant who recommends and leaves, or a staffing vendor who sends capacity for your backlog, an FDE owns the outcome: the system, the delivery, and the result.

Inside your team

They work through your pipeline, repos, and review process, on your real backlog. Not from a conference room.

System first

They set up your Agentic SDLC and the AI Intelligence Layer before shipping: one way of working, full visibility.

Delivery at AI speed

They ship production features from your backlog, with speed and quality measured together.

Proven, not promised

Every feature is priced in dollars and compared against your own baseline. You see the progress in your own numbers.

Why AI Teams Need Forward Deployed Engineering

You bought the tools. The team has the licenses. Delivery looks the same. Here’s what’s actually in the way.

Uneven AI Adoption

A few engineers ship at AI speed, the rest work like it’s 2022, so the tools are paid for but delivery never speeds up. Forward-deployed engineers install one way of working with AI instead of ten private workflows.

AI Fails on Established Codebases

AI shines on greenfield demos but stalls on established codebases: aging code, hidden business logic, weak tests, unclear ownership, long review paths, and outdated docs. The platform you actually run is where the tools help least.

No Proof of Real AI Adoption

AI changed what the old metrics can prove. Story points and velocity alone cannot show who ships with AI versus who just holds a license, what a feature costs, or whether AI is improving delivery.

The FDE Talent Gap

The missing skill goes beyond headcount: system design, Agentic SDLC, codebase work, delivery ownership, telemetry, and outcome accountability. That profile takes 6 months to hire and rarely sticks.
